Title: Liquid Love
Year: 2018
Status: completed
Story: This one went through countless iterations until I landed on the one that felt right.

[Verse 1]
I'm hungover from this breakup, 
I swear "never again" each time I wake up, 
but half wants to grovel back and makeup, 
though I know I should let it go and shape up.

[Verse 2]
When I sober up I don't forget the pain, 
but time lets my heart take over my brain, 
this straight and narrow is hard to maintain, 
when I get the urge it's hard to abstain.

[Chorus]
Your love's a shot that burns my veins,
a toxic chaser that binds my chains,
this lingering taste drives me insane,
There's nothing like poison to numb this pain.

[Verse 3]
Different people different kinds of boozy, 
some make me high some make me woozy, 
he makes me fly each time he knew me, 
she makes me smile as she seduced me.

[Verse 4]
I crave the drink called your affection, 
binging it all night to curb my depression, 
all I want is your undivided attention, 
till I wake up hungover from the rejection.
