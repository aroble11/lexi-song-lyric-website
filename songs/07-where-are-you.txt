Title: Where Are You?
Year: 2007
Status: work in progress?
Story: I don't even remember what this song was supposed to be about.

[Verse 1]
I'm wandering through a misty fog.
I keep seeing all these different faces,
they apparate in and out of my vision.
Everyone I've ever met, right before me.
But I don't see you.
I can't find you.
Where...

[Verse 2]
I'm enveloped in a tangible darkness.
Voices now - loud then quiet.
Resounding off the walls of opaque mist.
I'm so afraid right now - why can't I hear your voice?
Where have you gone? 
I can't hear you.
Where...

[Chorus]
Oh please, reveal yourself to me!
I need to see you, hear you, touch you - just know your presence is near!
Don't leave me alone like this, I can't bear it!
No, no, no more, oh please, tell me...
Where are you?!

[Verse 3]
Am I doomed to wander forever in here?
Searching for something that may or may not appear?
I refuse to be a fool, but I'd relent if it meant I could find you.
So why can't I see you?
Or hear you?
Where have you gone?
Where...

[Verse 4]
If you come back to me, I'll forgive everything!
I won't hold a grudge, I'll forget your entire past.
We can start over fresh, anew, with a blank slate.
Oh please, just a glimpse...
A whisper...
I love you, and that won't ever waver.
Where are you?!
