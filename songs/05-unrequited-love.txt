Title: Unrequited Love
Year: 2007
Status: completed
Story: I wrote this song for a fanfiction I wrote many years ago - it's one of the few that isn't actually about a real person.

[Verse 1]
Your lifeless eyes looked through me,
your thoughtless curses scarred me,
your heartless hands cut through me,
your coldness envelopes me.

[Verse 2]
Despite your icy bitterness,
flowing from empty golden eyes,
I'll always spare you tenderness,
and never leave you lonely.

[Chorus]
If only you could know,
oh how much I love you so,
and worry, watch, and wait,
when you arrive back home so late.
Oh, if only, if only,
you could feel the tears I shed,
from senseless fights and arguments,
resulting in you leaving,
and my sleeping alone in bed.

[Verse 3]
Though my love goes unrequited,
and cries of pain unheard,
my love for you is endless,
pure, unfiltered, flawless.

[Verse 4]
I hope one day you'll love me,
and show me with no doubts,
embrace me unobstructed, 
your warmth is all I wanted.

[Chorus]
If only you could know,
oh how much I love you so,
and worry, watch, and wait,
when you arrive back home so late.
Oh, if only, if only,
you could feel the tears I shed,
you could feel the tears I shed,
from senseless fights and arguments,
resulting in you leaving,
and my sleeping alone in bed.
