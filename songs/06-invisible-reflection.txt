Title: Invisible Reflection
Year: 2007
Status: completed
Story: One of my earliest works.

[Verse 1]
I'm not the girl you thought you knew,
I've got a life and so do you.
Your beliefs are your blindfold,
And mine are not ones you hold.

[Pre-Chorus]
Save me? I'm not being taken over.
Sugar and spice? Nothing but my cover.
It hurts to be trapped in dishonesty,
But I fear you'll remove my autonomy.

[Chorus]
My reflection's a deception,
An invisible impression.
The image of deceit,
For the standards I'll never meet.

[Verse 2]
With so much ease you believe my ruse,
Happy to stay blind to my false truths.
Oh how it'd hurt to tell the truth to you,
So I lie, I lie, I lie... To you.

[Bridge]
I'd hoped one day you'd love me,
And not just for what you see,
But for the person who I am inside,
The one that I've been forced to hide.

[Chorus - Reprise]
My reflection's a deception,
An invisible impression.
The image of deceit,
For the standards I'll never meet.

[Outro]
Oh how it'd hurt to tell the truth to you,
So I lie, I lie, I lie... To you.
