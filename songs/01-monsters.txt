Title: Monsters
Year: 2008
Status: complete
Story: This is one of the first songs I ever wrote. It started as just a poem with Verse 1 and the Bridge, but later I wrote the rest to flesh it out and formulate the structure into a song.

[Verse 1]
The mind can be a prison, where nightmares are the warden. 
Where your deepest fears and worries line this personal dungeon. 
At least with dreams, they fade and vanish the moment you awake, 
but what about the demons that cling to you past daybreak?

[Pre-Chorus]
In broad daylight they whisper lies, they cloud my head with all my doubts. 
The walls close in around me tight, I am trapped and I can't get out.

[Chorus]
I can't break free, 
from these chains I can't see, 
Why does my mind have to be my worst enemy. 
The angel on my right has given up on me, 
Cuz the demon on my left now lives inside of me. 

[Verse 2]
The face you wear in public only masks your private sorrow,
head held high for the facade but deep down your smile's hollow.
On the nightstand sits a bottle filled with little white capsules,
but is that enough to free me from the dungeon in this castle?

[Bridge]
So if there was ever a monster living beneath my bed, 
I'd shake his hand and let him stay, if he takes the one from my head.
