Title: I was your moon
Year: 2018
Status: completed
Story: I was broken when I wrote this. This is written more like a poem than a song, but it can be sung through like one.

I was your moon, reflecting all your light,
Gently in your orbit, your arms around me tight,
But when I was away you didn't feel so lonely,
Cuz truth be told I was not your one and only.

The Venus to your Mars, a perfect cosmic pair,
Everyday my heart and soul laid out to you bare, 
You claimed the stars, they sparkled in your eyes, 
But little did I know they were only pretty lies.

I tried to break free, but your grip was too tight,
You are gravity pulling me back with all your might,
It wasn't till you'd taken all I had achieved,
That you could finally let go, leaving me shattered and deceived.

When I finally did escape, I drifted off in space,
Lost and alone, and in a cold, dark place,
Floating through void, in endless, dark despair,
Yearning to reclaim the stars that you had made me share.

Your sunrise of a smile felt good for quite a while,
But now it reminds me of a love that turned vile, 
My old self a shell, no more part of me lingers, 
Now I'm only composed of what slipped through your fingers. 

I was your moon, you were my galaxy,
How could you betray me and leave me in agony? 
I was your moon, you were my Earth,
Why was I just another you thought you could hurt? 

I was your moon, but now I shine on my own,
I've found my worth, I no longer feel alone,
I was your moon, you were my everything, 
but now I'm gone, and soon you'll mean nothing.
